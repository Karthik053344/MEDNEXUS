# Security and clinical safety

Do not process identifiable patient information in this prototype.

Before real healthcare deployment:
- OIDC/SAML authentication and RBAC
- tenant isolation
- encryption in transit and at rest
- managed secrets
- immutable audit events
- retention/deletion policy
- secure object storage
- vulnerability and dependency scanning
- penetration testing
- model/data provenance
- subgroup performance analysis
- calibration and uncertainty evaluation
- external validation
- clinical safety/risk management
- human factors evaluation
- regulatory classification and quality management

The system should fail safely when inputs are incomplete, out-of-distribution, or ambiguous.
